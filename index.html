Hello, I’m Elena

I make characters that feel already alive.

I entered the world of doll art in 2020. A year later, my first original doll was born — and I have been following these characters ever since.

Every creation begins with an emotion. I work intuitively, paying attention to the smallest details until a face, gesture or piece of clothing reveals a personality. Each doll has a name, a character and a tiny destiny of its own.

For me, art is a conversation with the world: a way to share warmth, wonder and inspiration — and to touch the most delicate strings of the human heart.
